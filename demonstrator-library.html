MD115 and the onshore commission ban: provider viability

A representative private Australian higher-education provider run through twelve quarters while two real policies act together - Ministerial Direction 115, the visa-processing throttle tied to a provider's allocation, and the onshore agent-commission ban. Research funding · Australia

NCGP reform: the research workforce over the decade ahead

The Australian Research Council's National Competitive Grants Program, reformed on 22 July 2026 - fewer schemes, salary pathways for early-career researchers, a sharper national-priority alignment. The model reproduces documented ARC history from 2010 to 2024, then runs the reforms ten years forward against a near-flat real budget and a slow-building workforce.
