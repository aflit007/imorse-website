NCGP reform: what it does to the research workforce.

On 22 July 2026 the Australian Government announced reforms to the settings and design of the Australian Research Council's National Competitive Grants Program - a simpler scheme structure, salary pathways for early-career researchers, and a sharper alignment to national priorities. This is a live model of what follows.

A working demonstrator you can drive.

A complete, validated system-dynamics model of the NCGP - live in your browser, nothing to install. The left view reproduces documented ARC history from 2010 to 2024, so you can watch the model behave like the real program before trusting anything it says about the future. The right view re-anchors to today and runs the reforms ten years forward. Seven levers, seven charts, and two scenarios you can hold side by side. Select the forward view, then move a lever one at a time before combining them - the interesting behaviour is in the interactions.

How to read it. The model is calibrated to ARC and government data - the rising fellowship share, the near-flat-to-declining real budget, the observed career-stage shares, the growth in industry collaboration and Indigenous participation. Its behavioural relationships are the main uncertainty, and the most consequential of them are exposed as levers rather than buried in the code. Read shapes and turning points, not point values. A forward run is a scenario, not a forecast: it is an instrument for seeing how a conclusion depends on the reforms and on the assumptions you are willing to make. Where a number matters to a decision, treat it as directional.
